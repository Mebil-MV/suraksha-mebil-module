Transcript & health information

Eat a balanced diet, including Vitamin C-rich fruit and vegetables. Drink warm, non-alcoholic fluids regularly, and keep warm and dry. Avoid alcohol in cold weather. It widens blood vessels near the skin, increasing heat loss, and impairs judgment. This raises the risk of hypothermia.

The glowing warmth is a visual metaphor. Food supports normal immune function; it does not guarantee protection from infection.
