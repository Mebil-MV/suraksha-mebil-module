HOW TO PLAY

A little practice helps.

Start a round. Hold any object and slide your finger to the backpack. Release over the bag to pack it. You can also tap an object, then tap the bag or “Pack this item”. Open the bag to remove an item. Pack 8 essentials within the game’s 6 kg limit. Finish whenever you’re ready.

Each essential earns 12.5 points; each low-priority item costs 15. Your final score is rounded and cannot go below zero. A perfect kit earns the Kit Master badge. XP is earned only when you improve your personal best.

Challenge time keeps running while you look inside the bag, read help, or leave the tab. Choose practice for a relaxed round.
